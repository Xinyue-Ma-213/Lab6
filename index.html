My Anime List

Sword Art Online

A virtual-reality adventure in which players become trapped inside an online game and must fight to survive.

Why I recommend it

I like its combination of futuristic technology, fantasy worlds, and emotional relationships.

Sword Art Online

Sword Art Online explores how virtual worlds can affect real relationships and personal identity.

One Piece

A long-running adventure about Luffy and his crew as they sail across the world in search of a legendary treasure.

Why I recommend it

I recommend it because its world is imaginative, and every member of the crew has a personal dream.

One Piece

One Piece is especially memorable for its themes of friendship, freedom, and determination.

One Punch Man

A superhero comedy about Saitama, a hero who can defeat almost every enemy with only one punch.

Why I recommend it

I enjoy how the series combines exciting battles with humour and makes fun of superhero stories.

One Punch Man

Genos is one of my favourite characters because of his mechanical design and determination to become stronger.

The Disastrous Life of Saiki K.

A comedy about a high-school student with powerful psychic abilities who only wants to live a quiet life.

Why I recommend it

I recommend it because the contrast between Saiki's calm personality and his chaotic friends is very funny.

The Disastrous Life of Saiki K.

Saiki K. is easy to watch because its stories are short, fast-paced, and filled with unusual characters.

Fate

A fantasy series in which powerful Masters summon legendary heroes to compete for the Holy Grail.

Why I recommend it

I like its combination of historical characters, magic, complex conflicts, and animated battles.

Fate

The Fate stories present different versions of the Holy Grail War and give their characters difficult choices.

AIR

An emotional story about a travelling performer who meets a mysterious girl during one summer.

Why I recommend it

I recommend it because of its peaceful atmosphere and emotional story about memory, family, and separation.

AIR

The contrast between its bright summer setting and its sad story makes AIR especially memorable.
